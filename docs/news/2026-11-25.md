# BarkBot · Wednesday 25 November 2026

6 pull requests merged, three of them the News for Sunday to Tuesday ([#331](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/331), [#332](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/332), [#333](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/333)). BarkBot is rebuilt to match Grok Bot (the oracle), the app it is measured against, from captures of what Grok Bot 1.2.3 shows. Gate A is the check that compares our screens with those captures, case by case. All three other PRs changed Gate A's count: two changed how it scores, and one changed the app, which turned 33 of its cases green.

## What a person can now do

**The transcript is a named log, as on Grok Bot** ([#329](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/329)). In Grok Bot, the page's `<main>` has no name. The conversation inside it is a `role=log` named "Conversation transcript", and it takes focus. Ours had the name and the focus on `<main>`.

1. Now the transcript is that named log, and so is the empty conversation shown in its place. `<main>` has no name.
2. A screen reader finds the conversation under the same name as in Grok Bot.
3. A click on the transcript focuses the log. All 312 behaviour rules hold.

This is a name match only: it moves no box case.

**The pill face is a 24 by 24 picture** ([#329](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/329)). Grok Bot draws the face on the View conversation details pill as a 24 by 24 image. Ours is now an svg of that size with the tilted face drawn inside. It is scaled to fit the square from the tilt's angle, not from a number fitted to a capture. The faces in the rail and the details are unchanged. That picture is a box case on each of the 33 box screens, and all 33 went from missing to pass.

## What Gate A now measures

Tuesday's report counted 3,013 of the oracle's boxes that no case scored ([#326](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/326)). Now those that paint are scored ([#328](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/328)):

1. A box is a case when it is at least 20 by 20, outside the conversation, scored by no other case, and paints: a picture or background image, an svg with a drawn part, text of its own, a shadow, or a fill or top border that moves a pixel by at least 4 in 255 over what is behind it.
2. Each of Grok Bot's boxes pairs with one of ours anywhere on the page, the best overlap first. It passes within 1 pt on every edge. One of ours left over that paints is an extra.
3. That gives 530 box cases over 33 of the 54 screens. On Wednesday morning 462 passed. The pill face above made it 495 ([#329](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/329)).

The last PR taught the pairing three shapes ([#330](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/330)):

- A card's parts pair by overlap within their kind, so one part too many fails only itself.
- A text-less `<i>` is an icon glyph, as Grok Bot draws its icons, scored by its centre.
- A box painted only by its top border is that line, scored by its edge.

Four Connect apps icons went green, which made 499 of the 530 box cases, and one card part on two widths: 2,693 of 2,791 cases pass. No screen's count went down.

## What got harder to break

- A false green is gone: Grok Bot's 46 by 46 selected tile on the heart character shape had passed against our 36 by 36 glyph. It now fails, 5 pt off on each edge: we draw no selected tile ([#328](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/328)).
- An empty svg, which is what a broken icon looks like, no longer counts as painting ([#328](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/328)).
- The two transcript-focus rules now also need `role=log` on the focused element, not only its label, so the log above cannot quietly lose its role ([#330](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/330)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- The picture viewer's picture still fails: it sits 19 pt off ([#328](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/328)).
- 16 boxes in 9 screens are not scored: Grok Bot's captures do not hold the page colour behind them, so whether they paint is not known. They come back when Grok Bot is captured again ([#328](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/328)).
- The captures keep no outline, side or bottom border, `::before`, `::after` or mask, and no svg ink, so an svg is judged by its centre only. Opacity, clear text, gradients and shadows are filed ([#328](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/328)).
- Most of what still misses is named and is not ours to fix: 59 cases come from Grok Bot's own captures, where its list kept a stale row or its scroll. Others are placeholder bars where Grok Bot has copy, pictures we have no real content for, and three things our app does differently ([#330](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/330)).
- Connect apps' count went from 146 to 142 on purpose: its backdrop and dialog are among the 16 not scored, and four avatar rings the dialog's own colour do not paint ([#328](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/328)).
