# BarkBot · Monday 23 November 2026

13 pull requests merged. BarkBot is rebuilt to match Grok Bot (the oracle), the app it is measured against, from captures of what Grok Bot 1.2.3 shows. Four changed what a person can do: a picture viewer, file downloads, and a guard on which conversations open. Five made the oracle sweep, the script that drives Grok Bot to take captures, safer and Gate B more honest; three prepared captures; one tidied a fitted time row.

## What a person can now do

**The picture viewer** ([#322](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/322), [#315](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/315)).

1. In a conversation with five sent pictures, press the second tile. Our viewer opens at that picture, "2 / 5", where the tiles said "Open image full screen" and did nothing until Monday.
2. It covers everything right of the rail: the name, Mark up, Download media and Back to chat, the picture, and a strip of all five with the shown one ringed.
3. Press the dimmed +3 tile: the viewer opens at the third picture, "3 / 5". The strip reaches pictures 4 and 5.
4. Download media saves the picture under its sent name. Escape or Back to chat closes the viewer and gives focus back to the tile.

**A text file or PDF opens from its card** ([#317](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/317)). Press a sent file's card, or Enter on it; until Monday it was disabled. A text file or PDF downloads under its name and is never drawn in BarkBot's own page. What Barkpark stored as text stays a text download, even if it reads as HTML; any other type is refused.

**Only BarkBot's own conversations open** ([#320](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/320)). BarkBot reaches Barkpark with one token, which can open any conversation, and the page names the conversation. Now the file and event routes and the send, upload and answer functions first check that the conversation is on one of BarkBot's own computers and that a bot of BarkBot's own names it, or that this server made it. Anything else gets a 404 and nothing is sent; a read that fails counts as a no.

## What got harder to break

- Gate A: the two viewer captures went from 0 to 79 of 79 cases each; the pictures-sent and pictures-waiting screens did not move ([#322](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/322)). A time-row spacing fitted to one capture's time is gone, so the old total moved from 2,035 to 2,034 of 2,103, one named drop ([#309](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/309)).
- The sweep now holds the exact element it picked and presses only that one: a toast, a scrolled twin or a swapped tile under the pointer stops the sweep instead ([#318](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/318), [#321](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/321)).
- Gate B, the timing gate, judges on recorded local runs (63 of 63); hosted main runs read 60 of 63, the exit budget of 250 ms flipping at 255 to 389 ms on the runner. A hosted drop is now called runner timing only when every flipped case is tagged a clock, and two cases sharing one key stop the run ([#311](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/311), [#314](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/314), [#319](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/319)).
- Message actions on hover: two clean captures of Grok Bot 1.2.3 show nothing on hover, so there is nothing to build ([#310](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/310), [#313](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/313)). [#316](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/316) added the sweep recipes for the viewer's two captures.
- The behaviour rules stood at 296 at #320's run.

## Where it is slow or rough

- **For looks only.** Mark up does nothing, and the viewer's composer takes words and sends nothing. There are no arrow keys between pictures; none were captured ([#322](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/322)).
- The picture's own place in the viewer is not scored: ours sits 19 higher than the oracle's, and nothing captured explains why ([#322](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/322)).
- A PDF downloads instead of showing, on purpose: Chrome's PDF viewer would run a PDF's own script under BarkBot's origin ([#317](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/317)).
- After a restart, a conversation that was not kept does not open until its bot record is saved; one on a revoked computer does not open. A page can still start a conversation on a workspace-mate's computer from the workspace's list; only Barkpark can tell whose computer it is ([#320](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/320)).
- The sweep's hold has a gap of one round trip between its read and its mark, accepted for now ([#321](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/321)).
