# BarkBot · Friday 27 November 2026

11 pull requests merged, one of them the News for Thursday ([#338](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/338)). BarkBot is rebuilt to match Grok Bot (the oracle), the app it is measured against. Gate A is the check that compares our screens with captures of Grok Bot, case by case. Grok Bot is now at 1.3.0: two PRs moved the baseline to it, seven made BarkBot follow it, and one added a check.

## What a person can now do

**Labels in sentence case** ([#341](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/341)). Settings now reads Usage & billing, Sign out, Check spelling while typing, Follow system, Automatic updates, Check for updates, Add rule, Always ask and Allow without asking. Elsewhere: Connect apps, Password managers, Create new Bot and Edit avatar. 38 cases went from fail to pass, and none were lost.

**Library has a Search button** ([#342](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/342)), at the left of the panel's header, 36 round and drawn as Close is. Pressing it does nothing yet.

**Below 800 wide the details panel is closed** ([#343](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/343)).

1. Make the window narrower than 800. The panel goes, and the View conversation details pill says closed.
2. Press the pill. The panel opens, 208 wide at 720. A second press closes it.
3. Widen the window to 800. The panel is back, 288 wide.

45 cases went from fail to pass.

**Bot's computer while Unreachable** ([#344](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/344)). The Computer tile shows a small no-signal glyph where the screen face was. While Asleep, the face is back.

**The Voice picker** ([#345](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/345)). Its grid is 322 tall and runs to the dialog's foot, its cards are 138 wide, the picked card's rule is 38%, and the dialog grows 48 taller upward from its foot. The 28 voices of 1.3.0 were already there, in order. The picker's start and end states now pass in full, 54 of 54 and 41 of 41.

**The Language list** ([#346](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/346)). Rows are 14/20 and 32 tall, and the list is as wide as its widest label, Arabic (United Arab Emirates). All 33 label cases moved to pass.

**Get Grok Bot for mobile** ([#347](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/347)). The app's mark now sits above the code, and the dialog is 471 tall, 70 taller than before. 90 of its 92 cases pass; the 2 left are pictures we have no real content for.

## What Gate A now measures

- **The new baseline** ([#340](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/340)). 48 states were captured again on 1.3.0 and checked for personal data; the 6 send states stay on 1.2.3. A sticky, fixed or absolute box now counts as having an unknown behind it. Recorded once: 2,656 of 3,069, with every moved case named by its cause and each cause given a task.
- **Wider captures laid out fresh** ([#339](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/339)). With `--pin`, the capture sweep scrolls the transcript up once and down twice after each width step. Without it, 1.2.3's width captures kept old row places: 57 artifact cases.
- **Through the day**: 2,656 of 3,069, then 2,694 (#341), 2,739 of 3,055 (#343, where 14 extras of the panel at 720 and 790 stopped being cases), 2,739 of 3,054 (#344), 2,835 of 3,049 (#345), 2,868 (#346) and 2,877 (#347).
- **A scene must read a real conversation** ([#348](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/348)). On the 1.3.0 baseline a regeneration dropped the picture viewer's conversation, and the gate timed out with no word of why. A new rule in `node scripts/check.mjs` reds a scene whose conversation key is gone or names no captured state. 12 made-up cases pin it, and each planted break turns it red.
