# BarkBot · Monday 16 November 2026

15 pull requests merged. Sunday made BarkBot hold many bots; Monday went to what goes wrong when you use them: two bots in one folder, a message lost while you switch, a change lost when two happen at once. Most fixes came from reviews of the new bot code, each named in its pull request. Two more guarded the test runs, and one was this News page's own first version.

## What a person can now do

**Each bot works in its own folder** ([#62](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/62)). Press **Create** and name a bot. Its first conversation asks Barkpark for the folder `bot-` plus the first 8 characters of the bot's id, never its name. The Main Bot keeps the computer's shared folder. If the computer's chat host is too old to make folders, one plain line says so and nothing is sent.

- The Main Bot, when it moves in on first load, skips any conversation that has a bot folder ([#64](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/64)).
- Two of your bots never share a folder: Create checks your list and makes a new id, up to 8 tries ([#66](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/66)).

**Switch bots without losing a message.** Make a bot, type its first message, and pick another bot before the conversation opens.

1. The message goes once, to the bot you wrote it to; then the pick goes through ([#63](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/63)).
2. Pick the first bot again before the other's page loads: you go back to it. The last click wins, and the rail and the page show the same bot ([#67](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/67)).
3. If the first message fails, the page stays on that bot, says why and keeps the words, pictures and files in the box. After 6 s the pick goes through anyway, and the words wait in that bot's box.
4. Press **Create** instead of picking: the words are kept the same way, with a line saying "not sent" or "may have been sent" ([#79](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/79)).

**When things fail.**

- A send that fails keeps its words. If it may already have gone (a lost answer, a 500, a 502 or 504), the page says so instead of inviting a second send ([#69](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/69)).
- A new conversation that could not be saved to its bot says in one line that a reload may not open it again ([#73](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/73), [#75](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/75)).
- A rename and a new chat at the same moment, or from two tabs, both stay in the bot's record ([#74](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/74)).
- The online dot and the "unreachable" line describe the open bot's own computer, not the default one ([#72](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/72)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- A Barkpark read that hangs now costs a page load 8 s, once ([#70](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/70)). A normal read answers in well under 0.3 s.
- A change to a bot's record waits for the one before it, for at most 10 s; then it is called off with a line asking you to reload (#74). Barkpark has no per-field write, so BarkBot queues the changes itself.
- Two tabs pressing Create at the same moment can still pick ids with the same first 8 characters. The chance is 1 in about 4 billion; only a check in Barkpark would rule it out (#66).
- The bot folders were tested against a stand-in Barkpark, not in a live turn (#62).

## Across the projects

The bot folders use the chat sessions with a `bot_folder` that Barkpark merged on Sunday ([FRIKKern/barkpark#26451](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/26451)). The rule "a send that may have gone is not a refusal" (#69) follows from how Barkpark orders a send: the turn goes to the computer first, the message is stored after. The same day Barkpark ended a turn whose message could not be stored ([FRIKKern/barkpark#26632](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/26632)), and made BarkBot's session reads, up to 50 per page load, load no old messages ([FRIKKern/barkpark#26658](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkpark/pull/26658)).
