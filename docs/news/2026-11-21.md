# BarkBot · Saturday 21 November 2026

5 pull requests merged, one of them Friday's News. BarkBot is rebuilt to match Grok Bot (the oracle), the app it is measured against, from recordings of what that app does. All four others changed what a person sees, each rebuilt from the Grok Bot 1.2.3 captures read on Thursday: the account menu and Connect Apps, the Settings dialog, the conversation's cards and scenes, and time rows between messages.

## What a person can now do

**The account menu** ([#281](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/281), [#295](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/295)). Open it from your account button:

1. The menu is in three groups, with Upgrade plan under Weekly usage and a submenu arrow on Support. The usage line is a stand-in text of the captured length, with no real name, e-mail or usage.
2. Click "Get Grok Bot for mobile". The Get mobile dialog opens, as it does from Settings.
3. Press Escape. Focus goes back to the account menu's button.

**Connect Apps** ([#281](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/281)). The dialog has a Close button, a Mac filter, "Recommended for you" with the 1.2.3 apps, "This Mac" with a line for each app, Open settings on Location and a day tile on Calendar. Connect, Open and Open settings do nothing yet.

**Settings** ([#280](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/280)). Open Settings from the account menu and step through its pages:

- **General**: Change profile photo (a plain disc) and Send with ⌘↵, off.
- **Computer**: your device, "Last opened 13 minutes ago" with a grey dot; Messages with Read Texts and Send Texts, off; and Update Bot's Computer as a row with an Update button.
- **Permissions**: Approvals (Auto-review) in place of the Auto-Review switch, and App permissions with Open apps.
- **Updates**: version 1.2.3, "You're up to date".

In Gate A, every case on all four Settings screens now passes (General 88 of 88, Computer 82 of 82, Permissions 77 of 77, Updates 74 of 74). A new behaviour rule opens Settings from the account menu and checks each page.

**Time rows between messages** ([#297](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/297)). In a conversation, a message that comes at least an hour after the one before it, or on another local day, gets a time row above it: "Today 11:31 PM", or "Nov 15 1:10 PM" for an older day. A stored message uses the time Barkpark saved for it; one just sent uses the page's clock until its stored copy arrives. Messages 40 s apart get no row.

**Cards in the conversation** ([#295](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/295)). A file card with two files stacks them and numbers their names, "(1)" and "(2)"; in a narrow card the name gives way to its number. The details panel no longer closes itself in a window under 800 wide. An answered question card cut off at the top of the view keeps its answered choice.

## What got harder to break

- The behaviour rules grew from 251 to 257 over the day, and all 257 held on the day's last merge ([#297](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/297)). New ones open Get mobile from the account menu, walk the Settings pages, and check that rows 40 s apart get no time row while rows 3 h 10 min apart get one.
- A check with four cases makes sure a cut-off question card keeps its choice ([#295](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/295)).
- Gate A went from 1,522 to 1,977 of 2,103 cases over the day; the last merge added 24 and left every other screen as it was ([#297](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/297)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- **For looks only.** In Settings, Update, Open apps, Approvals, Check for Updates and Change profile photo do nothing ([#280](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/280)). In Connect Apps, Connect, Open and Open settings do nothing ([#281](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/281)).
- **The hour is a guess within bounds.** The captures show only that the gap for a time row is more than about a minute and at most 3 h 8 min; one hour sits between, unmeasured ([#297](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/297)).
- **Not tried live.** No live send to a real Barkpark was made for the time rows ([#297](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/297)).
- The Voice row's "Helix 1.0x" is still fixed in the code, so a picked voice is lost on reload ([#295](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/295)).

## Across the projects

Barkpark runs the chat API BarkBot uses. The new time rows read the time Barkpark stores with each message (its `inserted_at`) ([#297](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/297)).
