# BarkBot · Tuesday 17 November 2026

25 pull requests merged. Monday's bot code met its reviews: most of today's fixes are for when Barkpark is slow or an answer is lost on the way. Three moved BarkBot onto new Barkpark writes made the same day. Three added only rules (the checks the app must pass), two were about the News and the landing script, and one was about keyboard focus in the overlays.

## What a person can now do

**Send again without sending twice** ([#107](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/107)). Send a message whose answer is lost: the words come back with a line. When the next Enter would send the same words, pictures and files to the same conversation, it carries the first send's key, Barkpark runs it once, and the line says sending again is safe. Otherwise the line asks you to look in the conversation first. Edited words, other files or a key older than 23 h make a new key.

**Words are never lost.**

- A send that fails, or may have gone, gives its words back as their own paragraph before anything typed since ([#80](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/80)). They come back first even after a paste of an old message ([#208](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/208)).
- A new bot's first message whose conversation does not open within 10 s comes back to the box: "This message was not sent: its conversation did not open in time. Send it again." It is never sent later on its own ([#89](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/89)).
- A first conversation that could not be saved to its bot opens again when you come back to that bot ([#76](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/76)).
- The Bot name field is read-only while another bot's page loads, so a name typed then is saved to no bot ([#77](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/77)).

**Bots that keep their changes.**

- A rename and a new chat's conversation each set their own field of the bot's record, so neither undoes the other ([#90](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/90)). A rename on a slow but working Barkpark is saved ([#78](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/78)).
- Create writes only a key that is still unset. If another tab made a record there first, the dialog says nothing was written over it and asks you to try again ([#191](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/191)).
- If Barkpark does not answer a Create's save, or answers 502, 503 or 504, the dialog says the bot may already be saved: press Create again and it finishes that same bot ([#200](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/200), [#81](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/81)).
- The 50-bot limit counts only bots that show in the rail ([#82](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/82)).
- The rail (the list of bots down the left) puts the Main Bot alone in the main slot, with its badge ([#171](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/171)).

**Pictures and files.** A picture picked while another is still read never brings a sent one back ([#138](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/138)). When a send's pictures come back beside new ones and the box holds more than four, all stay, a line says four at most, and Enter sends nothing ([#154](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/154)). A file that cannot be read is named, once, and the rest of the pick is added ([#150](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/150), [#206](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/206)). A refused pick no longer hides the "may have been sent" line ([#165](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/165)).

**Settings.** Escape on Settings (account menu, then Settings) gives focus back to what had it before the menu opened, as Grok Bot does ([#186](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/186)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- The resend key lives in the tab's memory. A reload loses it, together with the words given back ([#107](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/107)).
- Changes to one bot's record go one at a time, per BarkBot server; a held change lets the bot go after 40 s at most. Two BarkBot servers at once were not tested ([#126](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/126)).
- The behaviour rules run against a stand-in Barkpark on hosted CI, not against a live one; the newest count is 230 rules ([#220](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/220)).
