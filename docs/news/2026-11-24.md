# BarkBot · Tuesday 24 November 2026

4 pull requests merged. BarkBot is rebuilt to match Grok Bot (the oracle), the app it is measured against, from captures of what Grok Bot 1.2.3 shows. Two closed a leak in the live status stream, one made the picture viewer modal, and one added a report to Gate A.

## What a person can now do

**The status stream shows only your conversations** ([#324](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/324), [#327](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/327)). Each conversation's live state, working, blocked or idle, comes to the page from one stream. Until Tuesday it passed on everything Barkpark's stream carries for BarkBot's token: every conversation in the workspace, or every one for an admin token, with its title, written from the conversation's words.

1. Now only BarkBot's own conversations go out: one this server made or proved, or one a bot record names on one of its computers.
2. Each goes out as its id and its state, nothing else, and only when the state is one of Barkpark's four words: working, blocked, idle or unknown.
3. Revoke a computer while the stream is open. Within 10 s its conversations stop going out.

A reconnect still resumes where it left off, and the replay is cut the same way.

**The picture viewer is modal** ([#325](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/325)). Open a picture from its tile.

1. Press Tab or Shift+Tab: focus goes round the viewer's controls and never leaves it. The rail, the conversation and the panel behind take no focus and no clicks.
2. Open the viewer's composer menu and press Escape. Only the menu closes; the viewer stays, with focus in it.
3. Close the viewer. Focus goes back to the tile, and the page behind works again.

## What got harder to break

- Gate A now prints, per screen, how many of the oracle's boxes no case scores, such as an unlabelled picture or a stage. From the captures: 3,013 boxes over 54 screens, most on New Bot (145), New Bot named (143) and Edit avatar (138). The viewer's own picture is one of the 66 on its screen. It is a report only; no record changes ([#326](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/326)).
- Five new behaviour rules: three for the viewer ([#325](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/325)) and two for the stream ([#327](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/327)).

## Where it is slow or rough

- Barkpark caps the stream's first snapshot newest-first at its sidebar's cap. With an admin token, BarkBot's own conversations can fall off it and read idle ([#324](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/324)).
- The 3,013 boxes are parts of Grok Bot's screens that no Gate A case scores; the report counts them and fixes none ([#326](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/326)).

## Across the projects

Barkpark runs the chat API and the status stream BarkBot reads. Its stream carries every conversation a token can see, so BarkBot now cuts it to its own before anything reaches the page, as Monday's guard did for opening a conversation ([#320](https://github.com/FRIKKern/barkbot/pull/320)).

No pictures today: BarkBot keeps pictures out of git.
